AINZ NATURAL GLOW SOAP WEBSITE
==============================

FILES
- index.html       Main webpage
- styles.css       Complete responsive design
- script.js        Mobile menu and scroll animations
- assets/          Put your real product photos here

HOW TO USE YOUR GOOGLE PHOTOS IMAGES
1. Open the shared Google Photos album.
2. Download the selected photos.
3. Rename them as follows:
   - product-main.jpg
   - product-box.jpg
   - product-closeup.jpg
   - product-lifestyle.jpg
   - ingredient-photo.jpg
4. Put the renamed files inside the assets folder.
5. In index.html, replace each placeholder <div> with an image, for example:

   <img src="assets/product-main.jpg" alt="AINZ Natural Glow Soap product" />

IMPORTANT ITEMS TO EDIT
- Verified ingredient names and descriptions
- Official product directions
- Price
- Facebook / Messenger link
- Contact number and email
- Regulatory or product registration information, if applicable

PREVIEW
Double-click index.html to open it in your browser.
